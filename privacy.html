Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Token Tape (the "App") is a private publishing tool operated by the owner of the Token Tape YouTube channel and the AI Daily site (yingchen066.github.io/ai-daily). It is used only by its owner to upload and manage videos on the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube API Services.

Data we access

With the owner's authorization, the App accesses the owner's YouTube channel to upload videos, set thumbnails, captions and metadata, read and manage comments, and read channel analytics. It does not access any other user's data.

How data is used and stored

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's private server, are never shared or sold, and are used solely to perform the actions above. No personal data of viewers is collected, sold, or used for advertising.

YouTube and Google

The App uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.
